See road distress the moment you spot it

Distress Detection is a Keras convolutional neural network trained to recognize common pavement distress from a single photo, no measuring tape or inspection software required. Upload a picture of a road surface and get back the distress type and its severity in seconds.

Classify an image

What it detects

Potholes

Bowl-shaped voids in the pavement surface, rated low, medium, or high severity.

Patching

Repaired sections of the road, flagged so crews can track repair quality over time.

Cracking

Longitudinal and transverse cracks running with or across the direction of traffic.

Ravelling & weathering

Surface aggregate wearing away from age, traffic, or exposure to the elements.

Rutting

Channelized depressions worn into wheel paths by repeated, heavy loading.

How it works

  1. 1

    Upload a photo

    Any JPG or PNG of a road surface, taken from a normal walking or driving distance.

  2. 2

    The model analyzes it

    The CNN grayscales and resizes the image, then classifies it against the trained distress types.

  3. 3

    Get your result

    See the predicted distress type and severity alongside your uploaded image.
