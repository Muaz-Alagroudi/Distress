Distress code legend

Five distress types, each rated low, medium, or high severity except rutting, which the model scores as a single class: 4 × 3 + 1 = the 13 trained classes above.

Code Type Description
01 Potholes Bowl-shaped voids in the pavement surface, rated low, medium, or high severity.
02 Patching Repaired sections of the road, flagged so crews can track repair quality over time.
03 Cracking Longitudinal and transverse cracks running with or across the direction of traffic.
04 Ravelling & weathering Surface aggregate wearing away from age, traffic, or exposure to the elements.
05 Rutting Channelized depressions worn into wheel paths by repeated, heavy loading.

Survey procedure

  1. 1

    Log a photo

    Any JPG or PNG of a road surface, taken from a normal walking or driving distance.

  2. 2

    The model analyzes it

    The CNN grayscales and resizes the image, then classifies it against the trained distress types.

  3. 3

    Get your survey record

    See the certified distress type and severity alongside your logged photo.
